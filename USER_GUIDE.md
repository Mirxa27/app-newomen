# Welcome to Newomen - Your AI Self-Discovery Platform

## What is Newomen?

Newomen is a revolutionary self-discovery platform featuring **NewMe** - your brutally honest AI companion who remembers everything about you. Unlike typical chatbots, NewMe never engages in small talk. Every conversation is either deeply therapeutic or playfully provocative.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 💬 Chat with NewMe

Talk to NewMe through text or voice. She's inspired by therapeutic approaches that cut through defenses and help you see the truth about yourself.

**What makes NewMe special:**
- **Brutally Honest**: No sugarcoating, just truth
- **Memory-Driven**: Remembers every detail you share
- **Therapeutic**: Asks questions that pierce through your defenses
- **Addictive**: You'll keep coming back for more insights
- **Personal**: Uses nicknames and creates real connections

### 🎙️ Real-Time Voice Conversations (NEW!)

Experience natural, flowing conversations with NewMe using advanced voice technology:

- **Speak Naturally**: Just talk as you would with a friend
- **Instant Responses**: NewMe responds in real-time with her voice
- **Visual Feedback**: See audio waveforms as you speak
- **Automatic Transcripts**: All conversations are saved for you
- **Smart Insights**: NewMe extracts key patterns and insights automatically

**How to use Voice Chat:**
1. Go to the Chat page
2. Click the "Voice Chat" tab
3. Click the phone icon to start
4. Allow microphone access
5. Start speaking!

### 📸 Photo Sharing

Share photos with NewMe for deeper context:
- Upload images directly in chat
- NewMe analyzes and remembers your photos
- Perfect for sharing moments, places, or visual inspiration

### 🎯 Assessments & Personality Tests

Discover yourself through:
- **Free Public Assessments**: Try 5-6 tests without signing up
- **Premium Assessments**: Access 20+ certified tests after login
- **AI-Guided Experience**: NewMe guides you through each assessment
- **Instant Results**: Get AI-generated insights immediately
- **Memory Integration**: Results are stored in NewMe's memory

### ⚖️ Balance Wheel

Visualize your life balance across 8 key areas:
- Career
- Relationships
- Health
- Personal Growth
- Finances
- Fun & Recreation
- Physical Environment
- Contribution

Rate each area from 1-10 and see your life balance visualized in real-time.
